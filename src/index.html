A TINY PROGRAM THAT LEARNS

Where Handwired comes in.

Meet Handwired ↗

First, find your sound. Rust measures the pitch and 16 harmonics in your recording—the ingredients that give a note its character.

Then, teach the synth. Handwired builds a little trainable program that mixes those harmonics into a wave. It measures the difference from your target and calculates how each harmonic should change. As it learns, the sound gets closer.

Finally, make a melody. The piano plays that learned sound recipe at different pitches. One vowel becomes a whole keyboard of notes.

The nerdy details ＋

Handwired’s Circuit combines 16 sine waves into 128 waveform samples, computes mean squared error against a phase-aligned target, and backpropagates that error. Gradient descent updates the 16 amplitudes; locked sliders stay fixed. Rust/WebAssembly handles pitch detection and harmonic analysis. Web Audio plays the result.

The tone-match percentage measures the simplified harmonic target. This captures the character of a steady vowel; speech, breath, consonants, and changes over time aren’t modeled. There’s no pretrained model, and no audio is uploaded.
