A PROGRAM THAT LEARNS

This instrument starts as a simple recipe for sound. Handwired lets us tune that recipe with gradients, until its harmonics approach yours. Freeze a few controls. Teach it again. Listen to what changes.

What’s actually learning? +

Sixteen harmonic amplitudes. We estimate a steady pitch and harmonic spectrum from your recording, then reconstruct a target with aligned phases. A Handwired circuit synthesizes a waveform and computes its squared error against that target. Its own backpropagation adjusts the amplitudes. Browser audio plays the resulting harmonic recipe at any note.

Sample vowels are synthesized examples. Spectral fit measures this simplified target, not voice-cloning accuracy. Breath, consonants, vibrato, and changes over time aren’t modeled. Nothing is uploaded.
